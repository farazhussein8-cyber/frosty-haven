This is Frosty Haven

“Dessert was never supposed to be boring.”

One small shop on Featherston Street, run by the two people who will almost certainly be the ones serving you. Nothing sits under a heat lamp. Nothing is pre-scooped. Every cup is swirled, stacked and finished while you watch — which is exactly why people keep saying the shakes are actually thick.

Halal, always. Generous to a fault. Open late enough to be a bad idea.
